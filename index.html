About

A bit about me

I'm a bilingual marketing and social media professional currently working as Social Media Coordinator at Prime Group, where I manage content, paid ads, and event marketing across 15+ hospitality and real estate properties.

My path took me from Caracas to Boston, where I studied Marketing with a minor in Economics at Bentley University, to a semester abroad in Milan, and now to Florida — a mix of places and experiences that shaped how I think about brands, culture, and connection.

Beyond the day-to-day, I love directing creative shoots and experimenting with illustration — always looking for the next creative project.
